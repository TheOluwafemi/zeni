How to play

  1. Flick

    Press a coin, drag back like a slingshot, and let go. The further you pull, the harder it flies.

  2. Hit exactly one

    Touch exactly one coin and you keep it, then go again with the same coin. Touch none, or two, and your turn ends.

  3. Mind the edge

    Any coin that falls off the table goes to your opponent. Tea cups block your path, but you can bounce off them.
